About

Built by one person who likes useful + playful software

MDSEO is the developer name of Andrei Burlac, an indie Android developer based in Longueuil, Quebec, Canada.

The apps share a common philosophy: stay small, respect privacy, avoid accounts and cloud lock-in where possible, and try to make something that feels a little different from the usual Play Store fare.

Whether it’s the deliberate constraint of only 36 film frames a week, the joy of shooting termites with watermelon seeds, or editing a PDF without uploading it anywhere — the goal is the same: make something people actually enjoy using.

Approach

How the apps are made

🔒

Privacy by default

Most apps declare zero data collection. Local processing is preferred whenever it makes sense.

🎯

Focused scope

Each app does a small number of things well instead of trying to be everything to everyone.

🎮

Playful constraints

Limits (36 frames, fake money, seed launchers) often create more interesting experiences than unlimited freedom.

📱

Android first

Built for the platform people actually carry. No forced accounts, no heavy SDKs when not needed.
